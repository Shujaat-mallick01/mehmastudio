Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about booking, payments, and delivery.

How early should we reserve our wedding date?

Peak Pakistani wedding season (October through March) books out 4 to 8 months in advance. We suggest reserving your dates as soon as your venue and dates are finalised to guarantee our team's availability.

What is the booking and payment schedule?

We require a 30% advance booking deposit to hold your date exclusively. 50% is due on the primary event day, and the final 20% is settled upon delivery of your high-resolution digital gallery and albums.

Do you travel outside Karachi or internationally?

Yes, we travel frequently across Pakistan (Lahore, Islamabad, Peshawar, Multan) as well as destination weddings abroad. Travel fares, transport, and hotel accommodations are billed transparently at actual costs.

When do we receive our photos and wedding film?

We share a sneak-peek set of edited photos and a social media reel within 7–10 days. The complete online gallery is delivered in 3 to 4 weeks. Once you approve your album photo selection, printed albums arrive within 2 weeks.

Can we customize our package?

Of course! If you have multiple nikkah ceremonies, extra dholki nights, or specific crew size requests, we are happy to prepare a bespoke proposal tailored to your vision and budget.
