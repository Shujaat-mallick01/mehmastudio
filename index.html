Our Story

Every family has a story. We are here to keep yours.

Mehma Studio began with one camera, one borrowed lens and a cousin's mehndi in Karachi. Ten years later we are a small team of photographers and filmmakers who still shoot every wedding as if it were in our own family.

We love the loud moments (the dhol, the baraat, the dance-offs) and the quiet ones just as much: a father's hand on his daughter's head, a nervous smile before the nikah. No stiff poses, no rushing. Just your day, the way it felt.
