Our Services

Comprehensive photography and cinematography for every Pakistani wedding tradition.

Crafted For Shaadis

Preserving every chapter of your celebration

From high-energy dholki beats to quiet, sacred rituals, we provide tailored coverage that honours your family's unique traditions.

Joy & Festivities

Mehndi & Mayun Coverage

Colourful outfits, rhythmic dholak singing, and spirited dance performances make the Mehndi one of the most exciting nights of any wedding. Our team focuses on candid guest laughter, the artistry of henna, and capturing dynamic dance choreography with natural lighting.

  • High-detail macro photography of bridal henna & jewelry
  • Full multi-angle video of dances and performances
  • Candid laughter, dholak sessions and guest candids
  • Fast turnaround for social media teaser clips

The Grand Ceremony

Barat & Nikah Ceremony

The Barat is the emotional core of the wedding journey. We capture the grand spectacle of the groom's procession, the sacred solemnity of the Nikah vows, and the deeply tender, respectful tears of the Rukhsati.

  • Groom arrival with dhol, fireworks and family welcoming
  • Intimate, respectful documentation of the Nikah ceremony
  • Organised and efficient family stage group portraits
  • Sensitive and touching coverage of the Rukhsati

Elegance & Grace

Walima Receptions

A night marked by regal poise, ambient floral decor, and joyful family gatherings. We conduct dedicated couple portraiture before the reception begins, followed by comprehensive coverage of visiting guests and heartfelt speeches.

  • Private, relaxed couple portraits prior to guest arrival
  • Stage and banquet hall decor, floral architecture and lighting
  • Extended family and VIP guest interaction photography
  • Speeches, dinner candids, and celebratory send-offs

Intimate Portraits

Pre-Wedding & Couple Shoots

Step away from the bustle of wedding days for an intimate session in Karachi’s scenic settings—from heritage architecture to golden hour beachscapes. Perfect for digital save-the-dates and wedding display prints.

  • Curated location scouting around Karachi
  • Natural direction that avoids forced or awkward posing
  • Multiple wardrobe and styling changes allowed
  • Delivered with priority turnaround for invites & save-the-dates

Moving Pictures

Cinematic Wedding Films

We treat every wedding film like a bespoke motion picture. Shot on cinema-grade cameras with pristine wireless audio recording for vows and speeches, each film is hand-graded and set to emotive, licensed music.

  • Cinema 4K camera gear with stabilised gimbals and drone views
  • Clear audio capture of nikah vows, elder blessings and speeches
  • 1-minute Instagram teaser reel delivered within 10 days
  • Full 5–7 minute highlight film + full-length documentary cut
